FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this actually available yet?

CareerFlow is in private beta. The pipeline you see on this page is a working design preview of the real app — sign up and we’ll email you the moment your invite is ready.

Where does my data live?

On your device. CareerFlow is local-first: no account required to use the core flow, no recruiter data sold, nothing synced to a server unless you choose to.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, and that’s deliberate. It captures listings and manages the follow-up — the applying stays your job. Automation that impersonates people is a line we won’t cross.

What does it cost?

Nothing while we’re in beta. When we launch, there will be a free tier and a paid one. You’ll never be locked out of your own data.
